Helpful adoption guides

Use these general topics to prepare for a conversation with a shelter or rescue organization. Requirements and available support vary by organization.

🏡

Prepare your home

Consider a safe, quiet space and ask the organization what supplies may be useful for the animal you are considering.

💬

Questions to ask

Ask about the animal’s known needs, daily routine, and any information the organization can share about its care.

📋

Understand the process

Check directly with the shelter about its application steps, timing, and adoption requirements.

🩺

Plan ongoing care

Talk with the shelter and a veterinary professional about routine care and the animal’s individual needs.

🐾

Consider your fit

Think about your household, schedule, budget, and experience when deciding whether adoption is right for you.

💗

Support local animals

Ask nearby shelters what help they currently need; options and volunteer opportunities differ by organization.

Every adoption is different.

This page is a general guide, not a shelter or adoption listing. Contact a local organization directly for current animals, services, and requirements.
